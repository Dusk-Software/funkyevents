It’s easy to fall for a photograph. Before you book a space, imagine the experience from the first arrival to the last goodbye.

## Picture the guest journey

How will guests arrive, find the room, and make themselves comfortable? Think about accessibility, weather plans, parking or transit, and whether the space works for the people you’re inviting.

## Ask what’s included

Venue packages vary. Ask what furniture, staffing, setup, cleanup, and equipment are included—and what needs to come from an outside vendor. The details help you compare the real cost of each option.

## Notice how it feels

Walk through the space at the time of day your event will happen. Pay attention to light, sound, flow between rooms, and where people naturally gather. Imagine your ceremony, dinner, speeches, or dancing in the space.

## Check the practical details

Before signing, ask about capacity, access times, vendor requirements, noise limits, weather plans, and cancellation terms. A planner can help you spot questions that are easy to miss when you’re excited.

The right venue doesn’t need to be the grandest one. It should make your guests feel welcome and give your celebration room to be itself.
